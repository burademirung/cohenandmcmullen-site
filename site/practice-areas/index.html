In short

What each practice covers, in a sentence or two.

What does the firm’s criminal defense practice handle?

If you are under investigation or have been charged, stop talking to investigators and call a defense attorney. Cohen & McMullen, P.A. tries criminal cases in Florida state court and federal court, from misdemeanors to serious felonies.

What does the firm’s commercial litigation practice handle?

Commercial litigation covers lawsuits between businesses, owners and partners: breach of contract, shareholder and partnership disputes, and business torts. Cohen & McMullen, P.A. litigates these cases in Florida and New York, including against large corporations.

What does the firm’s contracts & corporate formation practice handle?

The best contract is one that is never litigated. Cohen & McMullen, P.A. drafts, negotiates and reviews agreements and forms companies for businesses and individuals throughout Florida, with trial lawyers who know how contracts fail in court.

What does the firm’s wrongful death practice handle?

A wrongful death claim holds a person or company accountable when their negligence or misconduct causes a death. In Florida the claim is brought by the personal representative of the estate on behalf of the family, and strict filing deadlines apply, so families should speak with an attorney early.

What does the firm’s catastrophic injury practice handle?

A catastrophic injury is one that permanently changes a person’s life, such as a brain or spinal cord injury, amputation or severe burns. If another person’s negligence or misconduct caused it, you may be entitled to compensation for medical care, lost income and future needs.

What does the firm’s products liability practice handle?

A products liability claim seeks compensation from the companies that design, make, distribute or sell a product that injures someone because it was defective. Keep the product, its packaging and your receipts, and speak with an attorney before returning or discarding anything.
